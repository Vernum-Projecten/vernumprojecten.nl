All our offers and agreements are subject to the **NLdigital Terms 2025**.
These are the standard terms of the Dutch digital sector. NLdigital, the
trade association that draws them up, has deposited them at the District
Court Midden-Nederland, location Utrecht. We use the text unchanged; the
copyright rests with NLdigital.

## Download

[NLdigital Terms 2025 (PDF, 21 pages)](/voorwaarden/nldigital-terms-2025-en.pdf)

The Dutch original is on [the Dutch page](/voorwaarden/). In the event of a
dispute about the interpretation of the English version, the Dutch text takes
precedence. That is stated on the first page of the English version.

## How you receive them

You receive the terms as an attachment to every quote, before or at the
conclusion of the agreement, as
[article 6:233(b) and article 6:234 of the Dutch Civil Code](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0005289)
(Burgerlijk Wetboek) require. The file above is the same text, so you can
read it at any time.

What the quote or the agreement says takes precedence over the terms. Article
1.2 of the terms states that they apply only where the parties have not made
explicit written arrangements to the contrary. If you want to agree something
different on a point, we put it in the quote.

## What they contain

Chapter 1 always applies: applicability, offers, price and payment, duration,
confidentiality, intellectual property, liability (article 15), force majeure
and applicable law and disputes (article 18). Chapter 2, Compliance, and
chapter 3, Cyber security, also apply to every service. Chapter 4 governs the
processing of personal data.

The other chapters apply per type of service. For our work those are chapter
10, Advisory services, chapter 8, Development of software and websites, and
chapter 7, Software. Chapter 9, Maintenance of software and support, does not
arise with us: an assignment ends with the handover, as the page
[Services](@/diensten.md) says.
