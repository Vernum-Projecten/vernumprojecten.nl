This statement is about personal data: the data this site records and the
data you send us when you write to us. The rules come from the General Data
Protection Regulation
([GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)),
and where this page names an article, you can read it there.

## Who processes the data

The controller is Vernum Projecten B.V., established in Groningen, the
Netherlands, registered in the Dutch Commercial Register (Handelsregister)
under number 42164990. For a question about your personal data you reach us
at [privacy@vernumprojecten.nl](mailto:privacy@vernumprojecten.nl). The other
details are on the page [Contact](@/contact.md).

## What this site itself does

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics service, no visitor counter
and no advertising network. The pages contain no JavaScript, so there is
nothing to measure that is not named here.

Everything your browser fetches comes from this domain: the HTML, the one
stylesheet, the two font files and the images. No request goes to a font
service, a video service or a social network. You can check that: open the
network tab of your browser and look at which names pass by.

## What the hosting records

The site runs on GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of a visitor:
"When a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and
stored for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor has signed
into GitHub or not"
([GitHub Docs, What is GitHub Pages, Data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages)).
We cannot see those log lines and get nothing out of them.

GitHub, Inc. is an American company and serves this site from its own
servers. What GitHub does with that data is in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement);
the terms GitHub applies to data protection are among
[GitHub's privacy documents](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-data-protection-agreement).
If you want to avoid that processing, the only way is not to visit this site.
We are honest about that instead of showing you a choice screen that changes
nothing.

## What happens when you write to us

If you send an e-mail, we process your name, your e-mail address and what
you put in the message yourself. We use that to answer your question, to
make a quote and to carry out the assignment that follows from it.

The legal basis for that is article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR: the processing is
necessary to perform a contract, or to take steps at your request before
that contract exists. If your message is not about an assignment, we process
it on the basis of article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interest in being able to
answer whoever writes to us.

We keep your message as long as the conversation lasts. If you become a
client, the correspondence belongs to our records and the retention period
of seven years from
[article 52 of the Dutch General Tax Act](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320)
(Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen) applies. If you do not become a
client, we delete the message when the conversation has ended.

Your data does not go to another party, is not sold and is not used to send
you anything you did not ask for.

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to see which data we process about you (article
15), to have it corrected (article 16) or erased (article 17), to have the
processing restricted (article 18), to receive your data in a common file
format (article 20) and to object to processing based on a legitimate
interest (article 21).

Write for that to
[privacy@vernumprojecten.nl](mailto:privacy@vernumprojecten.nl). We answer
within the period article 12 of the GDPR sets. If we cannot resolve it
together, article 77 of the GDPR gives you the right to lodge a complaint
with the Dutch supervisory authority, the
[Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en).
